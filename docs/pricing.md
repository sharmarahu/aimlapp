There are no per-model tiers to reason about, and because DeepSeek is **prepaid**,
a runaway AI bill is impossible. You start free with your whole team and upgrade
only when you need more seats or AI credits.

## What 1 credit buys

Every AI action costs exactly **1 credit** — flat, DeepSeek-powered:

- A DeepSeek V3 chat reply, or a DeepSeek R1 reasoning reply
- An AI estimate suggestion on a ticket
- A standup digest synthesis
- A retro synthesis (with action items filed as tickets)
- Meeting-minutes generation
- AI ticket grooming / classification

> A 10-person team running daily standups + retros + ~30 AI chats/day typically
> lands around **~3,500 credits / month** — comfortably inside Pro's 5,000-credit
> pool. The Free tier's 200/month suits a smaller team or lighter AI use.

Credits are only deducted on **successful** responses — if a call fails, the credit is refunded automatically.

## The shared org credit pool

Every org draws AI usage from one shared monthly pool. Admins can optionally cap
individual members so no single person drains it:

```
Org Pool: 5,000 credits / month
├── Member A: quota 1,000 credits
├── Member B: quota 1,000 credits
├── Member C: no quota (draws from the shared pool)
└── Remaining pool: available to un-quota'd members
```

When the pool is exhausted, AI features pause for the org until the next monthly
reset or a top-up. Apex personal use never draws from an org pool, and vice-versa.

## Billing FAQ

**What happens when the pool runs out?** AI requests are blocked with a clear
message until the monthly reset or an admin top-up. Everything non-AI keeps working.

**Do unused credits roll over?** The monthly allowance resets each period;
admin top-ups don't expire.

**What counts as a request?** Each AI action is 1 credit, regardless of message
length.

**Is there a free trial for teams?** The **Free plan is the trial** — a full
15-person workspace with the AI Scrum Master, free forever. Upgrade when you
outgrow it.

**Why only DeepSeek?** One excellent, low-cost model kept the product simple and
the pricing honest — and because DeepSeek is prepaid, we can guarantee you'll
never get a surprise AI bill.
